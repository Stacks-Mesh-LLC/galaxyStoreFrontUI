The Essential Plus Kit pairs 1 Home Cellular System with 2 Voice Extenders and 1 Stationary Panic Button for broader whole-home coverage. It includes a basic pendant or necklace and connects you directly to the Galaxy Medical Alert Response Centre at the press of a button.

$54.95 per month with fall detection available for an additional $10.00 per month. Unlike traditional systems, this kit does not require a landline — it uses secure cellular (3G/4G/LTE) technology, with trained emergency operators available 24/7. The voice extenders and panic button place help within reach across more of the home, ideal for individuals with limited mobility.

Who it's for

Senior citizens and elderly individuals living independently
Individuals with limited mobility or balance issues
People recovering from surgery or medical procedures
Chronic conditions such as heart disease, diabetes, or respiratory issues
People living alone who want added peace of mind
Individuals with hearing or vision impairments
Anyone who may be at risk of falls or medical emergencies at home
