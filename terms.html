Terms & Conditions for Personal Emergency Response Systems (PERS)

Use of the Unit

In an emergency or when help is needed, press the button on the personal emergency response system pendant or base unit and a Galaxy Medical Alert Systems Ltd. Response Center representative will ask if you need assistance.

Testing of the Unit

Testing of the unit is not required. The unit is completely self-checking. However, Galaxy Medical Alert Systems Ltd. encourages subscribers to use or test the unit as often as desired. Subscribers are encouraged to press the button on the pendant or unit at least once a month, when the Response Center representative asks if help is needed, inform them that the unit is being tested.

Information Changes

To update subscriber information such as, phone numbers, addresses, and or responder information, contact Galaxy Medical Alert Systems Ltd. toll-free at 1-855-905-7414.

Payments

Private Pay Customers: While under contract, private pay customers are responsible for any financial charges and/or payments to Galaxy Medical Alert Systems Ltd. If you have provided your credit card information to Galaxy Medical Alert Systems Ltd., recurring charges will be made using that card. If you have provided bank account information to Galaxy Medical Alert Systems Ltd., funds will automatically be drafted from that account.

Agency/Government Customers: Payment for approved services has been arranged and contracted through the agency providing service. The subscriber is not responsible for payments on those approved services.

The Galaxy Medical Alert system has been fully checked over and tested before being shipped to you. It is the customer's responsibility to return the broken part(s) to Galaxy Medical Alert Systems Ltd.

Yes, the Galaxy Medical Alert system comes with a warranty. For as long as you subscribe to the Galaxy Medical Alert service, all parts are warranted under your service plan. Again, Galaxy Medical Alert Systems Ltd. will only cover normal wear and tear use. Should you have any questions, please call our toll-free number at 1-855-905-7414.
